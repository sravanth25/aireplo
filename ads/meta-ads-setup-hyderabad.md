# Meta Ads setup: Hyderabad launch

Goal: leads (free demo requests) for Aireplo from Hyderabad businesses, starting with real estate.

## 1. Before you start
- Facebook Page + Instagram account for Aireplo, both connected in Meta Business Suite.
- Payment method added in Ads Manager.
- Privacy policy URL for the lead form: https://aireplo.vercel.app/privacy (fill in the business name and address placeholders first).
- Special ad category: **None**. Aireplo sells software to businesses; it does not advertise homes. If Meta auto-flags the real estate videos as "Housing", request a review and explain it is a B2B software ad.

## 2. Campaign structure
Campaign: `AIR | Leads | HYD | Nov-26`, objective **Leads**, conversion location **Instant forms**, Advantage campaign budget **off** (budget per ad set while testing).

| Ad set | Ads | Daily budget |
|---|---|---|
| A. Real estate · Hyderabad | #02 English "1 AM lead", #03 Hinglish "Raat 1 baje" | ₹800–1,000 |
| B. Small business & D2C · Hyderabad | #01 "2 AM sale" | ₹500–600 |

Run 5–7 days without changes, then keep the winners.

## 3. Location
- **Hyderabad, Telangana + 25 km** ("People living in or recently in this location"). Covers Secunderabad, HITEC City, Gachibowli, Kokapet, Kukatpally, LB Nagar, Shamshabad side.
- Later, for real estate only, you can test pins with 5–8 km around the main building hubs: Gachibowli / Financial District, Kokapet / Narsingi, Kondapur / Madhapur, Kompally, Tellapur.

## 4. Audiences
Meta's interest names change; search each one in Detailed targeting and pick the closest match.

### Ad set A: Real estate
- Age **26–55**, all genders, all languages.
- Detailed targeting (interests, any of): Real estate · Real estate development · Real estate investing · Property developer · Real estate broker / agent · Construction · 99acres · MagicBricks · Housing.com · NoBroker
- **Narrow further (must also match):** behaviours "Facebook page admins" / "Small business owners" (if shown), so you reach builders and agents, not home buyers.
- Advantage+ audience: **off** for the first test (otherwise Meta drifts to home buyers).

### Ad set B: Small business & D2C
- Age **24–50**, all genders.
- Interests (any of): Small business · Entrepreneurship · Online shopping business / E-commerce · Shopify · Boutique · Fashion design · Instagram for business · WhatsApp Business
- Narrow further: "Small business owners" / "Facebook page admins".

### Later (after about 100 leads)
- Custom audience: upload your leads list, then a **1% Lookalike, India** filtered to Hyderabad.
- Retargeting: people who watched 50%+ of any ad in the last 30 days, with a "free demo" reminder ad.

## 5. Placements
Manual: Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, Facebook Reels, Facebook Stories, Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed. Turn off Audience Network and Messenger.

## 6. Instant form: "Aireplo – Free demo (HYD)"
- Form type: **Higher intent** (adds a review step, so fewer junk leads)
- Intro headline: "Get your WhatsApp AI demo"
- Intro text: "We set up the official WhatsApp Business API for your business: AI replies 24/7, lead follow-ups and a team inbox. ₹15,000 one-time, no monthly fee."
- Questions:
  1. Full name (prefilled)
  2. Phone number (prefilled)
  3. Business name (short answer)
  4. Business type: Real estate / Builder · Shop / D2C brand · Clinic · Coaching / Education · Other
  5. How many enquiries do you get per month? Under 100 · 100–500 · 500+
  6. When do you want to start? This week · This month · Just exploring
- Custom disclaimer checkbox: "I agree to be contacted by Aireplo on WhatsApp and phone about my enquiry."
- Thank-you screen: "Thanks! We'll WhatsApp you within 15 minutes." Button: **Call business** +91 76848 65330.

## 8. What to watch (first 5–7 days)
| Metric | Good sign | If it's poor |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3-sec views ÷ impressions) | 25%+ | Change the first 3 seconds |
| CTR (link) | 1%+ | Change headline / primary text |
| Cost per lead | Compare ad sets; keep the cheapest *qualified* one | Tighten audience or form |
| Lead quality | "Real estate / Builder" + "This week/month" answers | Move to Higher intent form, add a question |

Call or WhatsApp every lead within 15 minutes. Speed matters more than the ad.
