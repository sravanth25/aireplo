## How to submit (you do this; it needs your login)

1. **G2:** go to https://sell.g2.com/ and choose "Get listed". It's free. Verify with a work email on your domain; G2 may refuse gmail.com, so set up hello@aireplo.com first.
2. **Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice** are all Gartner Digital Markets, so one form covers all three: https://digitalmarkets.gartner.com/ and choose "List your product". The free listing is enough to start.
3. **SaaSworthy and Product Hunt** are free and fast, and they give useful backlinks.
4. **Reviews:** after each client handover, send a review link (G2 and Capterra both give one). Aim for 10 reviews in the first months, since listings rank by review count.
5. **Keep it honest:** use only real screenshots and real reviews. Don't post reviews yourself or pay for them; both sites ban that.
